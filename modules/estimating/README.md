# estimating

Phase 1 tender packages and quote comparison (product/10 EST-US1..6). Takeoff measurements follow in a separate PR.

- **Tables:** `tender_packages` (scope lines and linked documents as jsonb, allowances, award), `package_invites`, `quotes` (revision per company, EST-AC4), `quote_lines`. Money is decimal strings at the API and `numeric(18,2)`, with sums done in integer cents (`domain/tender.ts`).
- **Differences from schema/estimating.md:** quote status is `reading | ready_to_review | confirmed | superseded | declined` (what the web app needs; Nova extraction lands as `reading`), and allowances live on the package.
- **Access:** builder keys sit on the `commitments` tool (`packages.manage` and `quotes.review` need admin). `quotes.submit` is project membership; the service requires the caller's company to be invited, so a subcontractor only sees packages they were invited to (own invite only, no allowances or award) and their own quotes (EST-AC3).
- **Nova (F22):** `scopeForQuoteReading` (internal viewers only, package taking quotes) gives the `ai` module the trade and scope lines a PDF quote is read against. A quote saved from a Nova reading carries `aiSuggestionId` (builder staff only; stored in `quotes.ai_suggestion_id`, sets `source: ai_extracted`, and goes on `estimating.quote.received.v1`); the lines keep Nova's amount in `originalAmount` when the estimator corrects them.
- **Comparison (EST-AC6):** `GET …/tender-packages/{id}/comparison` (`quotes.review`, internal viewers only; a subcontractor gets 403, even for their own quote) levels the **confirmed** quotes (drafts, superseded and declined don't compete) in `domain/comparison.ts`: a column per quote, oldest received first, and a row per scope line, with cells `priced | provisional | missing`, the estimator's allowance for a gap, whether an exclusion names the line, and the quote's total, levelled total (total plus all its allowances, the figure `lowestQuote` ranks on), provisional sums, unmapped lines, exclusions and qualifications, and `validityExpired` against the project's local date. A price is flagged `high` or `low` when it is more than 25% from the median **and** at least $500 away, and only when three or more quotes price the line (or compete, for totals); the rule travels in the response as `outlierRule`. A scope line a quote doesn't price is `missing` even if an exclusion or allowance already covers it; the screen shows those beside it. Pure arithmetic on confirmed data: no model call, no credits. Comparing against the estimate arrives with draft estimates (phase 2).
- **Cost library and estimate sheets (EST-US10, EST-US11):** `cost_items` (org-level, unique code) with append-only `cost_rates` (region, `effective_from`); an estimate prices a line from the latest rate on or before its base date, its own region first, and copies the rate onto the line so later library changes never move a draft silently. `estimates` + `estimate_lines` (sections hold items, one level). Arithmetic is `domain/estimate.ts`: BigInt cents, quantity × rate per line, then contingency, margin (markup on cost, or a share of price) and GST, rounded half up. Nothing is Nova's: a missing rate is flagged and left out, never invented, and `issue` refuses (422 `estimate.rates_missing`) until every item is priced. A line can be tied to takeoff measurements; its quantity is the sum of the **confirmed** ones, live on a draft and frozen at issue (unconfirmed marks add nothing and are reported as `pendingMeasurements`). Library keys are org-scope for members (`estimating.library.*`); estimate keys sit on `commitments` at standard, `estimates.issue` at admin.
- **Not yet:** issued estimate to budget (financials, phase 3), draft estimate from the library by Nova (F24, flags only), CSV import/export of the library, estimate-versus-quote outlier flags in the comparison, invitation emails and access-link submission for non-users (X45), the comparison/outlier endpoint (the web computes it), CSV scope import, takeoff, sending takeoff quantities to scope lines.
