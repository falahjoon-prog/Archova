# Risk register (v1, 21 Nov 2026)

Owner: Sam (Security). Reviewed by: Grace (Compliance) for R-17 to R-21. Next review: Feb 2027 or when a module/supplier is added.

## Method

Likelihood (L) 1 rare, 2 unlikely, 3 possible, 4 likely, 5 almost certain, over the next 12 months. Impact (I) 1 minor, 2 moderate (one tenant inconvenienced), 3 serious (one tenant's data exposed or SOPA deadline missed), 4 major (several tenants, regulator notice), 5 severe (platform-wide breach, loss of trust or licence to operate). Score = L x I; residual is always shown as L x I. **Inherent** assumes no treatment; **Residual** is after the controls marked Implemented today (not designed ones). 1-6 low, 8-12 medium, 15-25 high. High residual risks need a dated treatment plan and John's acceptance; medium ones are accepted by Sam and reported; low ones are monitored.

Scores are the author's judgement from the code and docs and are for Sam and Grace to challenge. Status of a control follows the vocabulary in the README.
